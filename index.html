Questions Partners Ask

Who can partner with Metal?

Accelerators, funds, advisors, coaches, connectors, founder communities and venture studios.

What do my founders get?

Investor intelligence, warm-intro mapping through your network, a pipeline that runs across rounds, and Richard AI as a round analyst. Partner programs also unlock a founder deal. Techstars founders, for example, get a free founder-led demo, an extended trial and 50% off all plans.

Do I have to change how my program runs?

No. speedrun founders use Metal inside a 12-week sprint, and Goodwater brings it in after a round closes. Metal fits around your timing.

What happens after I reach out?

The partnerships team sets up an intro call, then helps you onboard your founders.
